hey team,

i'm a backend-leaning engineer working where ai and infra meet, \textasciitilde 2 years in, currently at xane ai. my favourite spot is where product, ai, and infra all intersect --- figuring out what's actually worth building, then building it so it doesn't fall over. i like designing the system architecture, the schemas, the failure-catching mechanisms. agents write a lot of my code now. i make sure it's the right thing and that it holds.

\heading{what i've done}

\textbf{xane ai}, built an ai agent on mastra that acts as an assistant for automobiles and appliances --- from information retrieval to service booking and warranty claims --- with full traceability, logging, and evals driving continuous improvement. built a document ocr pipeline ingesting 100k+ pages via mineru vlm, powering rag over qdrant with rrf hybrid retrieval (sparse + dense). designed system architecture, database schemas, apis, and aws deployment plans for a product serving 3.1m+ users.

\textbf{roger yc s24}, built an ai advertising agent solo --- takes a brand's url, scrapes and learns the brand's identity and tone, then generates creatives and live-avatar video ads with gpt image 2 and seedance 2, and ships them straight to meta ads. url to live ad, fully automated.

\textbf{otomashen}, built a high-throughput edi/x12 engine handling 20k+ claim files and millions of encrypted records --- drove a 166\% revenue increase. dragged api latency from \textasciitilde 10s down to under 1s with query tuning, indexing, and caching at scale. built a real-time dispatch system for proximity-based doctor matching, request lifecycle (accept/decline), and instant notifications. delivered 4 production-grade client systems, leading one end to end as lead developer --- architecture, implementation, deployment.

\textbf{stealth yc startup}, founding product engineer --- owned the architecture, schema, frontend, apis, and deployment of an ai work-management platform handling 100k--200k requests a month.

\heading{things i've built on my own}

\textbf{reachtoyc}, an ai agent for cold outreach to yc founders: pick a batch and it finds every founder and their likely emails, researches each company on the web, and drafts a personal email from your own prompt, resume and portfolio, sent from your own inbox. runs fully locally, free and open source: \lnk{https://reachtoyc.vercel.app}{reachtoyc.vercel.app}

\textbf{findmejob}, a go + wails desktop app with 100k+ jobs scraped from wellfound, greenhouse and more without public apis, plus your local agents (claude code, codex, opencode) to research companies, find founders and write the email: \lnk{https://findmejob.xyz}{findmejob.xyz}

\textbf{insightplus}, an open source event analytics engine handling tens of thousands of events/sec: \lnk{https://insightplus.shobhnik.site}{insightplus.shobhnik.site}

\textbf{whooper}, a proxy-layer caching engine for apis that caches responses and auto invalidates them with custom rules: \lnk{https://whooper.shobhnik.site}{whooper.shobhnik.site}

\textbf{amigmi}, a fun tool to track all your ai coding spend: \lnk{https://amigmi.xyz}{amigmi.xyz}
